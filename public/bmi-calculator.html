BMI Calculator

Check your Body Mass Index and understand your weight category

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple screening tool that uses your height and weight to estimate whether you are in a healthy weight range. While BMI does not measure body fat directly, it is a useful starting point for understanding your health status. A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy for most adults. Values above 25 indicate overweight, and above 30 indicate obesity — both associated with increased risk of chronic diseases. Note that BMI may overestimate risk in muscular individuals and underestimate it in those with low muscle mass.
