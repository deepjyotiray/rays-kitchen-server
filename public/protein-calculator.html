Protein Intake Calculator

Find your daily protein target in seconds

Protein is the most important macronutrient for body composition. Whether your goal is fat loss, muscle gain, or general wellness, getting the right amount of protein is essential. This calculator uses your body weight, activity level, and goal to give you a science-backed daily protein target. Most people need between 1.2g and 2.2g of protein per kilogram of body weight. Athletes and those in a calorie deficit benefit from the higher end of this range to preserve muscle mass and support recovery.
